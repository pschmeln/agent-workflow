What you do, in order

  1. Flash prototype/firmware/uno_r4_wifi/uno_r4_wifi.ino to an Arduino UNO R4 WiFi. In Arduino IDE pick that board, install ArduinoBLE 2.1.0, and use the UNO R4 board package 1.6.0. The sketch opens serial at 115200 and prints Cane demo controller and Advertising CaneDemo as a BLE peripheral.
  2. Wire four external drivers. D2 is hazard, D3 is unavailable, D4 is the route demo, D5 is the link. Do not power an actuator from a header pin.
  3. Install Bluefy yourself from the App Store. Open this HTTPS page in Bluefy on the iPhone 16. A laptop is not the runtime.
  4. Read section 1. Then try camera alone, model alone, and Bluetooth alone. Then turn on section 5.
  5. With a sighted spotter, check the physical outputs listed at the bottom. Do not cover anyone's eyes. The cane you already use stays the cane.

What a spotter can check

  • Before the phone connects, D5 uses the fast outage pattern and D2, D3, and D4 stay off. Serial prints link=outage.
  • After Connect, D5 changes to a short blip about every 2 seconds. Serial prints accepted source= and an increasing seq.
  • Manual D2 pulses only the hazard driver. Manual D3 pulses unavailable. Manual D4 pulses the route driver. None of those means the path is clear.
  • Switch the phone away from Bluefy, or turn Bluetooth off. Within about a second D2, D3, and D4 go off and D5 returns to the fast pattern.
  • Come back and tap Connect if it does not reconnect. Section 5 stays off until you turn it on again.
